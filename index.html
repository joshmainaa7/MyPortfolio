APPROACH

Finding it is only the first part.

A weakness only counts if someone can reach it. My work is proving which ones can be — and then getting them closed.

  1. 01

    Detect and monitor

    Continuous visibility across systems and networks using SIEM platforms — Wazuh, Rapid7 and Qualys — tuned to surface genuine signal instead of alert noise.

  2. 02

    Prove exploitability

    Hands-on web exploitation and reconnaissance with Burp Suite, sqlmap and Kali Linux to confirm whether a weakness is actually reachable in context.

  3. 03

    Investigate incidents

    Digital forensics and triage with Autopsy — reconstructing what happened, what was touched, and what the real blast radius was.

  4. 04

    Manage vulnerabilities

    Assessment and prioritisation across the stack, including bug bounty hunting — ordering the work by what is reachable rather than by raw severity score.

  5. 05

    Secure the infrastructure

    Hardening and administration across AWS Cloud, Kubernetes, Linux and server configuration — securing the ground the software runs on.

  6. 06

    Keep systems running

    Two years of frontline technical support: incident management, system diagnostics and debugging — security that survives contact with real users.
